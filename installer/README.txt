PERFORMLIVE BETA (Testing)  {{VERSION}}
Amanorsac Studio - amanorsac.studio

WHAT THIS IS
A stage instrument for running stems, loops, pads and a click live,
with section jumps you can queue mid-song. This is a test build.

THIS BETA ENDS
You can use this build until {{LAST_DAY}}. After that it stops
opening. Your projects and library stay on your computer.

INSTALLING
Windows   Run PerformLive-{{VERSION}}-Windows.exe and follow the installer.

WINDOWS SAYS "WINDOWS PROTECTED YOUR PC"
This test build is not signed yet. Choose "More info", then
"Run anyway".

WHAT GETS INSTALLED
  Application   C:\Program Files\Amanorsac Studio\PerformLive\
                (or the folder you choose in the installer)

YOUR PROJECTS AND SETTINGS
  Projects and library   Documents\Amanorsac Studio\PerformLive\
  Settings and logs      %LOCALAPPDATA%\Amanorsac Studio\PerformLive\
Uninstalling does not delete these.

NO ACTIVATION
There is no key and no account. PerformLive itself never connects
to the internet.

NO SOUNDS INCLUDED
The app opens empty. Bring your own stems, loops and pads.

SELL YOUR LOOPS AND STEMS
To become a PerformLive creator, email amanorsac@gmail.com or find
@studioamanorsac on Instagram. The STORE page in the app has a QR
code for the creators' WhatsApp group.

UNINSTALLING
Windows   Settings > Apps > PerformLive > Uninstall
